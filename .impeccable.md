## Aesthetic direction — "Espresso & bronze"

Warm dark, tactile, made-of-something. A well-worn leather-and-iron gym in
Reykjavík, or a workshop wall — not a fitness app dashboard. Refined rather
than aggressive: strength as _endurance_, not strength as intimidation.

Refinement preserves this world. Replacing it is a redesign and needs the
owner's agreement first.

### Palette

Espresso surfaces (OKLCH, warm hue ~57, never pure black), one true accent in
**bronze** with enough chroma to read as metal, and one cool anchor — a heavily
muted **slate** inherited from the crest's original navy, used as a surface and
never as a glow. Tokens are defined in `src/styles/global.css` under `@theme`.

### Typography

- **Fraunces** (variable serif) for display, set at magazine scale. This is the
  main differentiator: fitness sites are almost universally geometric sans, and
  a serif signals patience and permanence — which is the actual promise.

  > **Documented deviation — do not re-litigate.** The Impeccable detector
  > flags Fraunces under `overused-font`. The call is to keep it, deliberately:
  > that rule targets convergence across AI-generated web design generally,
  > whereas the competitive set here is Icelandic personal trainers, where a
  > serif is genuinely differentiating. It is also incumbent identity, it has
  > verified Icelandic coverage (þ ð æ ö á), and its variable italic carries
  > the hero's emphasis word. Swapping it would mean re-subsetting a woff2 with
  > full Icelandic coverage and retuning the whole scale — real cost against a
  > speculative benefit. Reconsider only as part of a deliberate rebrand.

  Fraunces is for **display and editorial** only: headings, the coach passage,
  the ForDads statements, the hero proof numerals. Not navigation, not
  controls, not UI labels.

- **Hanken Grotesk** for body and for all numerals.
- **Orbitron** for the wordmark and all-caps labels _only_. It draws a slashed
  zero, so `01` renders as `Ø1` and prices as `9.9ØØ` — it must never set
  digits.

### The crest

`public/logo/crest-*.webp` — a single continuous line drawing of a father
flexing with a child on his shoulders, alpha-masked so it recolours at any
scale via `--crest-color`. Use it at **architectural** scale: bleeding off
edges, embossed into surfaces. Never as a small logo sitting politely in a
corner.

## Explicitly avoid

Neon-on-black, glassmorphism, glowing accents, stock gym photography, progress
rings, transformation-challenge energy, anything that reads as SaaS.

## Hard constraints

- WCAG AA contrast minimum; verified per-element, not assumed.
- All motion degrades under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Fonts must cover latin-ext for full Icelandic coverage.
- **No photography of Aron or any client exists.** The design has to hold on
  typography, the crest, and texture alone — and must not leave holes shaped
  like a missing photo.
